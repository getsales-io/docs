<Update label="2026-11-26" description="A mailbox moves between sender profiles, a workspace sets its own tracking domain, and the signature belongs to the sender profile">
  **A mailbox moves with its conversations.** `POST /api/sender-profiles/{sid}/mailboxes/{emailAccountSid}/move`
  with `to_sender_profile_sid` hands a mailbox to another sender profile of the workspace. It stays connected,
  and its conversations go with it. A connect that meets a mailbox another sender profile holds answers `409`
  `mailbox_bound_elsewhere` with `context.sender_profile_sid` and `context.email_account_sid`, so the move is
  one call away. A new mailbox reads `status` `connecting` from its sign-in until its first sync ends.

  **A workspace sets its own tracking domain.** Three operations under
  `/api/sender-profiles/{sid}/mailboxes/{emailAccountSid}/tracking-domains`: `create` registers a subdomain and
  answers the one CNAME record to add, `{emailTrackingDomainSid}/verify` checks it, and
  `{emailTrackingDomainSid}/delete` removes a domain no mailbox is set on (`409` `tracking_domain_in_use`
  otherwise). The list answers each domain's `dns_records`, and `PATCH` on the mailbox sets
  `email_tracking_domain_sid`, `track_opens` and `track_clicks`. The steps are in
  [Set up a tracking domain](/kb/sender-profiles/tracking-domains).

  **Tracking and the unsubscribe link need a verified tracking domain.** A workflow whose email step holds
  `{{unsubscribe_url}}`, `unsubscribe_link` or a true track flag is not published while no mailbox of its
  sender profiles has a verified domain: `POST /api/workflows/validate` and
  `POST /api/workflows/{sid}/publish` answer `tracking_domain_required`. A due email that holds the link
  waits, with `result.held` on its task, until a domain is verified, and an email that forces tracking from a
  mailbox without one goes out untracked (`result.output.sent_untracked`).

  **The signature is the sender profile's.** `PATCH /api/sender-profiles/{sid}/mailboxes/{emailAccountSid}`
  no longer takes `signature_html`. A signature is `variables.signature` on `PATCH /api/sender-profiles/{sid}`
  (HTML, up to 8,192 characters) and goes into an email only where it is placed: `include_signature: true` on
  `POST /api/messages/send`, or `{{sender.signature}}` in an email step's body. A workflow email that its
  mailbox signed before this date goes out unsigned until its step carries `{{sender.signature}}`.

  **A due email waits for its mailbox.** An email step whose mailboxes are paused, or still in their first
  sync, waits instead of failing: up to 24 hours for a first sync, then `mailbox_first_sync_unfinished`. A
  LinkedIn step due while a new account's first sync runs waits too, and
  `POST /api/sender-profiles/{sid}/limits` answers `result.initial_sync` meanwhile. Its `result.linkedin`
  rows no longer carry `batch_size`.

  **The accounts read names what it could not read.** `POST /api/sender-profiles/available-accounts`
  answers `unreadable_workspaces`, a workspace whose read failed, and each account's `provider` and, while a
  LinkedIn account syncs, its `initial_sync`.
</Update>

<Update label="2026-11-26" description="Cc, Bcc, files and a later time on a message, text versions and files on workflow steps">
  **A message takes copies, files, a mailbox and a time.** `POST /api/messages/send` takes `cc` and `bcc` (up
  to 5 addresses each: `bcc` left out sends the mailbox's standing copy, `[]` sends none), `attachments` (up to
  5 files, each named by an https address, 20 MB together: documents, pictures, video, audio or a zip),
  `new_thread.email_account_sid` (the mailbox a first email leaves from) and `include_signature`. Every
  message answer carries `cc`, `bcc`, `include_signature`, `reply_to` and `hold_reason`.

  **A scheduled message pauses when the lead answers first.** It stays `queued` with `hold_reason`
  `lead_replied`, the thread's timeline gets a `scheduled_paused` event, and a task opens for whoever scheduled
  it. `PATCH /api/messages/{sid}` with a new `scheduled_at`, or `null` for now, sends it.

  **A text step holds two to five versions.** A `linkedin_send_connection_request`, `linkedin_send_message`,
  `linkedin_send_inmail` or `email_send` step takes `versions`: each with its `id`, `label`, `weight`,
  `enabled` and text, in place of the step's own text fields. A person draws one version when the step is
  planned and keeps it. A task carries `version_id`, `POST /api/workflow-tasks/search` filters on it, and
  `GET /api/workflows/{sid}` with `include` `step_stats` answers each version's numbers and which one leads.

  **LinkedIn steps carry files, and an email step's copies read a column.** `linkedin_send_message` and
  `linkedin_send_inmail` steps take `attachments`: up to 5 files of the workflow's library, 20 MB together, and
  one library file may now be 20 MB where it stopped at 10. An `email_send` step's `cc` and `bcc` take an
  address or one column reference such as `{{people.owner_email}}`.

  **A task says which touch it is and what it waits for.** Every workflow task carries `touch_seq`, and a
  scheduled one names what holds it in `result.held`. `variables_snapshot` is the step's text as rendered
  when the task was planned, not at dispatch.
</Update>

<Update label="2026-11-26" description="Webhook events for connection requests and sending caps, and message events that name the person">
  **Four new events.** `linkedin-connection-requests.sent` and `linkedin-connection-requests.accepted` fire
  when a sender profile's request goes out and when the person accepts it, with the person, the sender
  profile and the workflow step that sent it. `sender-profiles.limit-reached` and
  `sender-profiles.limit-released` fire when a daily cap of a sender profile's LinkedIn account closes and
  opens again, with the `limit_type` and the `cause`: `filters.where` on `payload.cause` keeps a subscription
  to LinkedIn's own refusals. The catalog holds 37 types, and
  [Webhook events](/guides/webhook-events) lists each payload.

  **Message events name the person.** `messages.sent`, `messages.received` and `messages.bounced` carry
  `person_sid` and `sender_profile_sid`, so a receiver matches a message to its person without a second call.

  **The limits read answers the account's health.** `POST /api/sender-profiles/{sid}/limits` with
  `snapshot: true` adds `result.snapshot`: the LinkedIn account's warmup score, what took it off, and tips.
</Update>

<Update label="2026-11-26" description="A data subscription, AI SDR licenses and vendor credits on a plan, and paid actions priced in dollars">
  **A plan takes three more lines.** `POST /api/billing-subscriptions/quote` takes `data_subscription` (its
  tier, with the three tiers priced in the answer), `ai_sdr` (licenses, one workspace's AI SDR each),
  `vendor_credits` (dollars, paid once) and a `promo_code`.
  `POST /api/billing-subscriptions/{sid}/change` takes `data_subscription` and `ai_sdr`, answers typed
  `steps` with what each charges, and never buys vendor credits (`422` `vendor_credits_by_checkout`). A
  subscription allocation takes `ai_sdr`, and `include` `entitlements` on a team or a subscription answers
  what the plan grants. The first LinkedIn bracket, 1 to 4 accounts, is $59 an account a month.

  **The AI SDR needs a license.** In a workspace whose plan holds none, turning an agent on, a test, a retry
  and a plan's approval answer `402` `ai_sdr_not_in_plan`. The agent still labels replies. A trial holds one
  license.

  **A member with Billing Manage changes the plan.** A change, a pause and a cancel are open to the buyer and
  to anyone holding `can_manage_billing` in the workspace the plan feeds. A subscription split across
  workspaces stays its buyer's alone (`403` `not_subscription_owner`).

  **A credit is five cents, and a paid action costs what its vendor charges.** The balance is kept in US
  dollars, and every answer that speaks credits counts one credit as $0.05. An AI call, a found email and an
  email check are charged at the vendor's price: the 20% added to AI calls and the rate of 25 credits a
  dollar are gone. A read of LinkedIn data on our managed infrastructure costs 0.1 or 0.5 credit, and a read
  answered from saved data or by one of the workspace's own LinkedIn accounts is free. An Enrich column takes
  `payload.params.trust_cache_max_age_days` (1 to 3650, 30 when left out, 0 for none), and saved answers are
  free up to the plan's allowance. Prices are in
  [Plans, credits and billing](/kb/workspace/plans-credits-and-billing#what-spends-credits).
</Update>

<Update label="2026-11-25" description="An AI SDR that is off does nothing, and a scenario's level decides whether a message is sent or drafted">
  **`off` stops the agent.** An agent whose `state` is `off` labels each new reply and does nothing else: it
  plans, writes and sends nothing, and its plans wait where they stand. Set to `on` again, it goes on from
  there, and a reply that came meanwhile is read as if it had just arrived, unless it is older than the
  agent's recency window. Until this date a paused agent went on planning and answering.

  **The scenario's level decides at the send.** On a thread with no mode of its own, a message the AI SDR
  writes goes out by itself when the latest labelled reply resolved to a scenario on `autopilot`, and waits
  as a draft on `copilot`, whatever the workflow's or the agent's own mode says. A thread's
  `agent_mode_override` still comes first, and a thread whose workflow stops or continues on a reply is left
  alone.
</Update>

<Update label="2026-11-10" description="A workflow copied to another table, who sent a message, and finders that read the row">
  **A workflow can be copied, into its own table or another one.** `POST /api/workflows/{sid}/duplicate`
  makes a draft copy with the steps, the sender profiles that still exist, the rotation, priority,
  schedule, `on_reply` and the AI SDR pick. The copy has no audience, no version and no history, and
  its steps are numbered again from 1. `playtable_sid` homes it on another table and `name` names it
  (`{{name}}` stands for the original's name). In another table a `run_column` step takes that
  table's action column of the same slug, or comes over with `field_sid` null when there is none.
  `result.missing_columns` lists every column the steps read that the copy's table lacks, with the
  steps that read it. With `dry_run: true` the call answers that list alone and writes nothing, so you
  can see what a copy would miss before you make it.

  **A message says who sent it.** Every message the messages API returns carries `sent_by`, the
  identity that called the send: `actor_type` `user` with a member sid is a teammate writing in the
  inbox, `api_key` with a key sid is an API key, `agent` with a member sid and `actor_name` set is an
  app (an OAuth connector) sending for that member, and `agent` with an AI SDR sid (`ub_ag_...`) is
  the AI SDR on its own turn. It is null on a message nobody sent from GetSales (an inbound one, one
  imported from the channel, a workflow's or a play's send, which `automation_ref` names) and on the
  messages sent before this change. The server stamps it and no write changes it.

  **An email finder reads the row.** A waterfall step of an email finder (`in:{provider}.email.find`)
  in a column's `payload.steps` can take empty `params`: the save fills `full_name`,
  `linkedin_nickname`, `company_name` and `domain` from the row. Send a key only to read another
  column, or null to switch that input off.

  **A sender profile's numbers count every send.** `POST /api/sender-profiles/stats` and
  `POST /api/sender-profiles/{sid}/performance` count the sender profile's own sends and visits,
  whoever made it send: a workflow, a play, a teammate in the inbox, or the account itself. They
  counted only the sends a workflow made before, so an account that worked from the inbox read zero.
</Update>

<Update label="2026-11-08" description="A Stop that holds, a daily limit that waits, and searches that say what they could not match">
  **A Stop on a column or a table stops the column.** `POST /api/playtable-runs/cancel` with
  `field_sid` or `playtable_sid` also turns `config.auto_run` off on the action columns it stops and
  sends back the cells queued in their auto-run queue, so new rows do not start them again a minute
  later. The answer adds `backlog_cells_count` (those cells, also counted in `reverted_cells_count`)
  and `auto_run_off_field_sids`; a `dry_run` names the columns it would turn off. A cancel by
  `run_sid` stops that run alone, as before. To run a column by itself again, update its `config`
  with `auto_run: true`, sending the whole `config` back.

  **A daily credit limit waits instead of failing.** A cell of an `auto_run` column that meets the
  column's `daily_credit_limit` before it spends anything stays `queued` with `error_msg`
  `daily_credit_limit_reached` and runs after 00:00 UTC, when the day's spend starts over. On a
  column without `auto_run` it still ends `error` with the same reason.

  **An AI search that could not match a word stops and says which.** When a word of an `ai_search`
  description matches no search value, the source pauses after planning with `status_reason`
  `filter_unresolved`, and `payload.plan_warnings` names the words. Resuming it searches without
  them; other words mean a new source.

  **Web search is priced as research.** `POST /api/playtable-fields/estimate-credits` prices an Ask AI
  column with `web_search` on the results each search reads back as input, and such a call may take up
  to 240 seconds. The column's `tool_sid` for `action_gtm_tool` lists every read it takes, the
  latest posts and the full profile among them.
</Update>

  channel from a message (a reaction, a delete on LinkedIn). In Playtables the metrics, suggest and
  estimate helpers, a column's and a table's cancel routes, and act and draft on an activity. In
  Signals a site's key pages and tracked events, which the site's own record carries now, and the
  signal preferences. In Integrations a single delivery's retry (the replay below takes a window) and
  the OAuth completion step.

  **New in the reference.** `POST /api/workflows/{sid}/add-people` adds people to a workflow in one
  call, and a person the workflow already holds stays as they are. `POST /api/people/{sid}/move` moves
  a person to another table. `POST /api/playtables/{sid}/archive`, `unarchive` and `duplicate`.
  `POST /api/playtable-fields/estimate-credits` prices a run before it starts, and
  `POST /api/playtable-runs/cancel` stops runs. The sender profile variables (four operations),
  `POST /api/agents/fix`, `POST /api/provider-connections/{sid}/crm-metadata` and
  `POST /api/webhooks/{sid}/replay`.
</Update>

<Update label="2026-11-08" description="Bulk edits on a table, and the Stop's numbers">

  lists: `add_tags`, `remove_tags` and `replace_tags` (`params.tag_sids`), `set_field_value`

  answers `422` with the field named, so a run that cannot do its work never starts.

<Update label="2026-11-08" description="A file a message carried can be fetched on demand">
  **Bring an old file in.** A file GetSales does not hold (a message from before the sender
  profile connected, a message of your own team sent from the channel's app, or a fetch that gave
  up) can be asked for: `POST /api/messages/{sid}/fetch-attachment` with the file's `index` puts it
  back on its way, one call per file, one browser action of the sender profile's budget on
  LinkedIn. The row comes back with the file `pending`. It lands minutes later and
  `messages.attachments-changed` says so. A voice message is transcribed once it lands. A stored
  file, a GIF, a file past the cap or one the channel refuses answer `attachment_not_fetchable`.
</Update>

<Update label="2026-11-08" description="Sender variables: one text for a pool of senders">
  **Write a text once.** A workflow step's text and a play's message read the sender profile they
  go out as: `{{sender.first_name}}`, `{{sender.last_name}}`, `{{sender.full_name}}`,
  `{{sender.label}}` and every variable your team keeps, `{{sender.<slug>}}`.

  **Team variables.** `POST /api/sender-profile-variables` adds a name for the workspace, and
  `PATCH /api/sender-profiles/{sid}` takes each sender profile's `variables`, merged by slug.
  `POST /api/sender-profiles/variable-usage` says which workflows and plays use one.
</Update>

<Update label="2026-11-08" description="Webhooks filter on the payload and replay an outage">
  **Filter on the payload.** `filters.where` delivers only the events that pass a condition on
  the payload, and `["*"]` subscribes to every event of the catalog, later ones included.
  The [webhooks guide](/guides/receive-webhooks) has the grammar and the limits.

  **Replay.** `POST /api/webhooks/{sid}/replay` puts the failed and platform-cancelled deliveries
  of a window back on the wire.

  **Reads.** `delivery_stats` gives a webhook's 24-hour count and last outcome, `include webhook`
  attaches a delivery's subscription, the search sorts by the failure columns and filters on
  `filters.account_sid`, and every delivery carries `occurred_at`. A test send can dry-run a filter
  on a `sample_envelope`, and a mailbox's history import no longer fires `conversations.created`
  for its old threads.
</Update>
