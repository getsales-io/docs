A GetSales workspace runs on a plan: a data subscription with LinkedIn sender profiles, email inbox capacity and the
AI SDR, and vendor credits bought beside it. This page covers the trial and the Free plan, what a plan buys and costs, what credits pay for, invoices,
and what happens when a payment fails.

<Tip>
**Give this to your AI assistant** (with the [GetSales connector](/kb/ai/connect-your-ai-assistant)):

"Using the GetSales connector: read this workspace's subscription, what it holds and when it renews, the credits
available and what they went on this period by feature, and the last three invoices. Then quote what adding two
LinkedIn sender profiles would cost per month. Change nothing."
</Tip>

## What does a GetSales plan buy?

A plan holds a data subscription and adds LinkedIn sender profiles, one email inbox package and the AI SDR as you
need them. Vendor credits are bought beside it, once. There is no charge per team member, and no charge per message
or connection request sent.

| Resource | What it is | When it is full |
|---|---|---|
| **LinkedIn sender profiles** (priced as LinkedIn accounts) | Room for the LinkedIn accounts your sender profiles connect. The cloud browser, the proxy and the limits come with each | A new LinkedIn account is refused with `402` `linkedin_sender_profiles_full`: "All 5 LinkedIn sender profiles of this workspace's plan are in use. Detach a LinkedIn account you no longer need, or add LinkedIn sender profiles in Settings, Billing." (the plan's own number). A plan with none: "This workspace's plan has no LinkedIn sender profiles. Pick a plan in Settings, Billing." |
| **Data subscription** | The base of a plan: **Startup**, **Sales team** or **Agency**. **Buy resources** marks it **Required** ("The platform, and our data within a day's limits, shared by the workspaces on the plan.") | An order with none is refused: "A plan needs a data subscription: pick one." A plan bought before 26 November 2026 has none and runs on the Startup limits free until its first change |
| **Email inbox capacity** | One package of 100, 500, 2,000 or 10,000 inboxes for the mailboxes your sender profiles connect. The package is one dedicated email server of its size for the workspace, set up after the purchase, usually within minutes. Each mailbox takes one inbox on a server ([Connect a mailbox](/kb/sender-profiles/connect-a-mailbox)) | A new mailbox is refused with `402` `email_inboxes_full`: "All 100 inboxes of this workspace's plan are in use. Detach a mailbox you no longer need, or add inboxes in Settings, Billing." A plan with none: "This workspace's plan has no inboxes. Pick a plan in Settings, Billing." A full email server answers `402` `email_server_full`, and a workspace with no server `409` `email_server_missing`. In the app, **Add mailbox** says it in its **Email server** field, with **Add inboxes** or **Buy email** |
| **AI SDR** | One license runs the AI SDR of one workspace ("The agent's work: its scenarios, Playbook, plans, follow-ups, hand-offs and the calendar it books on. Its model calls are paid from vendor credits.") | Without a license the AI SDR only labels replies, and turning an agent on is refused with `402` `ai_sdr_not_in_plan`: "This workspace has no AI SDR license. Add the AI SDR in Settings, Billing." |
| **Vendor credits** | Dollars bought once, from $50, for enrichment, AI columns, contact finding, AI searches, Creator and the AI SDR's model calls ("AI, email finding and live data at the vendors' prices; kept while the subscription runs."). The app shows them as credits: $50 is 1,000 | Paid work pauses until credits are added ([what stops](#what-happens-when-credits-run-out)) |

A plan whose recurring lines come to 500 a month or more, in its own currency, includes the Business service: a
quarterly review, one custom feature a quarter, a dedicated team chat and white label. From 1,500 it includes the
Enterprise service: everything in Business, white label and the dedicated team chat included, plus a monthly growth
review with dedicated customer success, one custom feature a month and migration. The month counts the data
subscription, LinkedIn, email and the AI SDR. Vendor credits count toward neither.

Over the API or MCP: [`search_billing_subscriptions`](/api-reference/id/billing_subscriptions/search-billing-subscriptions)
reads a subscription and what it holds.

## How much does a plan cost?

A plan costs the sum of its lines, a month, before tax: a data subscription ($29, $99 or $399), LinkedIn sender
profiles from $59 an account (1 to 4) down to $16 (100 to 187), one email inbox package from $59 (100 inboxes) to
$499 (10,000), and the AI SDR at $99 a license. Vendor credits are paid once, from $50. The **Buy resources** window
prices every order on the server before you pay.

**Data subscription.** **Startup** $29, **Sales team** $99 or **Agency** $399 a month, one per plan. The window lists
what each tier holds in a day: Startup 5,000 enrichments, 50 searches and 1,000 results a search, for one workspace.
Sales team 30,000, 200 and 10,000, for one workspace. Agency 150,000, 1,000 and 100,000, for any number of
workspaces.

**LinkedIn sender profiles.** The bracket your total lands in sets the price of every account:

| LinkedIn accounts | Per account a month |
|---|---|
| 1 to 4 | $59 |
| 5 to 9 | $39 |
| 10 to 19 | $29 |
| 20 to 49 | $25 |
| 50 to 99 | $22 |
| 100 to 187 | $16 |
| 188 to 300 | A $3,000 package for up to 300 |
| Over 300 | $3,000 plus $10 for each account over 300 |

So 4 accounts cost $236 a month and 5 cost $195. A plan bought before 26 November 2026 keeps $69 an account on a
line of 1 to 4 accounts until that line is changed.

**Email inbox capacity.** One package per workspace: 100 inboxes $59, 500 inboxes $119, 2,000 inboxes $249 or
10,000 inboxes $499 a month.

**AI SDR.** $99 a license a month ("The AI SDR for this workspace"). One license runs the AI SDR of one workspace.

**Vendor credits.** Paid once, in whole dollars from $50 ("From $50, any whole dollars"), with no discount and no
promo code. $50 adds 1,000 credits to the workspace's balance. In EUR the figure is the same and in GBP nine tenths
of it, and the balance takes the dollars ("£45 paid, $50 on the balance").

- **Periods**: **Monthly**, or **12 + 2 months**: you pay 12 months and get 14.
- **Currencies**: USD, EUR at the same figures, and GBP at 0.9 of each USD line.
- **Tax**: "Prices before tax. The payment provider adds the tax of your country at checkout."
- **Payment**: card, PayPal, Apple Pay or Google Pay, through the payment provider's checkout. GetSales never sees
  the card number.

Over the API or MCP: [`quote_billing_subscription_order`](/api-reference/id/billing_subscriptions/quote-a-billing-order)
prices an order without buying it. It takes `data_subscription`, `linkedin`, `email`, `ai_sdr`, `vendor_credits` and a
`promo_code`, and answers the three tiers of the data subscription priced.

## How does the trial work?

Every new account's first workspace starts on a 7-day trial, and no card is needed. On its first page the app asks
**How do you want to start?** ("Both give you 7 days of the whole plan. The card decides the vendor credits and what
happens on" the trial's last day):

- **Start without a card**: $5 of vendor credits (100 credits). The trial ends on its date, and outreach stops then.
- **Add a card**: $25 of vendor credits (500 credits), and $0 until the trial ends. Then the plan shown under the two
  options starts, monthly: by default a Startup data subscription, one LinkedIn account for each account connected
  (at least one) and the AI SDR, $187 a month with one account. **Edit plan** changes it, and a cancel before that
  date charges nothing.
- Either way the trial holds 10 LinkedIn sender profiles and an AI SDR license, with no email inboxes, no email
  server and no white label, so a first campaign sends on LinkedIn. **Add mailbox** reads **No email in your plan yet**, with **Buy email**, and a
  mailbox connect over the API is refused (`402` `email_inboxes_full`: "This workspace's plan has no inboxes. Pick a
  plan in Settings, Billing.") ([Connect a mailbox](/kb/sender-profiles/connect-a-mailbox)).
- Email comes with an email package of a paid plan, whose purchase replaces the trial.
- One trial per account. A workspace you create later starts without one and is suspended until it gets a plan.
- **Settings**, **Subscription** shows **Trial** with the date it ends, **Add a card** and **Buy now**. Until two
  days before the trial ends it also reads "Add a card by" that day "and get $20 in vendor credits." After that it
  reads "Add a card to keep it running.", and a card adds no credits. **Buy now** opens **Buy resources**, ends the
  trial and bills at once. With a card on, it reads "Your plan starts on" the date, with **Cancel the plan**, and
  another plan cannot be bought meanwhile: "This trial already has a card, and its plan starts when the trial ends.
  Cancel that plan in Billing first to buy another now."
- 3 days before the end, the owner is told by email and in the app: "Your trial ends in 3 days".
- The trial's credits expire when its 7 days end, even when a plan replaced the trial earlier, and they are
  spent before the plan's.

Buying a plan during the trial replaces the trial at once. When the trial ends without a plan, the workspace moves
to the Free plan and the owner gets "Your trial has ended": nothing is deleted.

## What is the Free plan?

The Free plan is what a workspace runs on after its trial or its plan ends. The workspace stays active and keeps
all its data, with no deletion date.

- **It holds** no LinkedIn sender profiles, no inboxes, no AI SDR license and no credits.
- **It keeps running**: tables, formulas, imports and the workflows' own steps, among them the API call step that
  calls tools with your own keys.
- **It holds back** LinkedIn and email: the LinkedIn accounts and mailboxes are parked, nothing new connects and
  nothing is sent. Workflows keep running, but their LinkedIn steps fail as they come due (`subscription_required`:
  "The sender's account is on hold: no subscription covers it right now.") and so do their email steps
  (`sender_profile_has_no_mailbox`: no mailbox can send). A failed step stops that person's journey and does not wait
  for a plan
  ([why a workflow step failed](/kb/workflows/troubleshooting#why-did-a-workflow-step-fail-and-how-do-you-fix-it)).
  A message sent from Unibox (**Messages**) is refused (`402` `subscription_required`), and the AI SDR waits
  (**Waiting: subscription needed**). **Settings**, **Subscription** puts it as "Playtables and workflows keep
  working, LinkedIn and email wait for a plan."
- **Credits**: none are granted, and none can be bought without a plan: "Vendor credits come with a plan: add a card
  to the trial or buy a plan first."

Buy a plan and LinkedIn and email come back within its numbers, with its credits. The workflow steps that failed
meanwhile go out only when you run them again
([which fix to use](/kb/workflows/troubleshooting#which-fix-to-use-retry-send-now-skip-or-restart-from-top)). Credits
that expired with an earlier plan do not return. **Settings**, **General** shows the plan as **Free**, also during the
trial.

## What is a credit worth, and how do credits arrive?

A credit is $0.05: 20 credits to a US dollar. The workspace's balance is kept in US dollars, and the app shows it,
and the price of each paid action, in credits. An AI call, a found email and an email check cost what their vendor
charges, and a read of LinkedIn data on our managed infrastructure costs 0.1 or 0.5 credit. The 20% that was added
to AI work, and the rate of 25 credits a dollar, ended on 26 November 2026. Credits come three ways:

1. **Bought once**: **Top up** (on the subscription card, on **Usage**, or under **Credits** in the sidebar) opens
   **Top up vendor credits** for a workspace on a paid plan: whole dollars from $50, and $50 adds 1,000 credits
   ("Paid once, in dollars. They land on this workspace's balance within a minute of the payment."). A new plan can
   take vendor credits in the same order.
2. **The trial**: 100 credits, or 500 with a card.
3. **A plan bought before 26 November 2026** that holds monthly credits keeps its monthly grant.

How long they last:

- **Plan credits roll over**: the plan's grants, and credits bought while a paid plan runs, stay for as long as the
  workspace is on a paid plan, and are lost when it leaves one (a cancel at the period's end, or the payment provider
  ending the subscription after a failed payment). A pause and the 3 days of grace keep them.
- **Credits bought before 26 November 2026 with no paid plan** last 90 days from the purchase.
- Credits with an end date are spent first, the soonest first.

The balance shows under **Credits** in the sidebar ("Credits available", "Used this period", "Next refill") and in
**Settings**, **Usage**. When the credits left fall to a tenth or less of the credits granted, the workspace's owner
is told once a period, by email and in the app.

Over the API or MCP: [`get_credit_lots_metrics`](/api-reference/id/credit_lots/credit-wallet-balance) reads the wallet.

## What spends credits?

Paid work spends credits: data reads, searches and AI calls. Sending, a workflow's waits, conditions and API calls,
and CRM syncs do not.

| Action | Credits |
|---|---|
| A work email found | The finder's price for each address found: 0.5 with Hunter, 0.4 with Findymail, 1.46 with Anymailfinder. A miss is free. An address the verifier rejects is charged too, the find and its check |
| An email verified | 0.064 a check with EmailListVerify, 0.4 with Findymail |
| An Enrich lead read (a LinkedIn profile) | Free from saved data, and free when one of your sender profiles' LinkedIn accounts reads it. On our managed infrastructure: 0.1 at **Basic**, 0.5 at **Advanced** |
| An Enrich company read (a company's LinkedIn page) | Free the same two ways. On our managed infrastructure: 0.1 at either level |
| A person's latest posts | Free when one of your sender profiles' LinkedIn accounts reads them, 0.1 on our managed infrastructure |
| An AI search | 0.5 per page of results that returns somebody (up to 25 people or companies), nothing for a page that returns nobody. Building its filter is one AI call plus 0.1 for each term it looks up, such as a place or an industry. A term already looked up in the past week, or one that matches nothing, costs nothing |
| Find people | 0.1 per company where somebody is found, nothing where nobody is, and 0.1 more, once per company, to look up its LinkedIn id when the row has only its page or website |
| Post engagement read on our managed infrastructure | 0.5 per page of 50 people who engaged, and 0.1 to 0.5 for each read that finds the posts. Free when one of your sender profiles can read posts |
| Use AI columns, Creator, a workflow's **Ask AI** step, dictation and voice-message transcripts | The AI model's own price, at 20 credits per US dollar. A typical Use AI row (about 1,000 tokens in and 300 out) costs about 0.1 credit on Claude Sonnet 5 and under 0.01 on the default model, Claude Haiku 5.5. A web search the model runs adds 0.2 |
| The AI SDR | What each turn's model calls cost, at 20 credits per US dollar ([what the AI SDR spends](/kb/inbox/ai-sdr#what-does-the-ai-sdr-spend)). The AI SDR itself needs a license on the plan |
| A read of a person the row knows only by their profile link, such as Latest posts | 0.1 more when their LinkedIn id has to be looked up first |
| An Enrich company read of a company known only by its domain | Up to 0.02 more: one web lookup for its LinkedIn page when GetSales does not already know it, charged whether or not the page is found |

Free, at 0 credits: LinkedIn messages, connection requests and emails sent, adding people to a workflow, CRM reads and
writes, the API call step and your own tools, a miss and a failed call, and Creator's own turns in the
[**Getting started** chat](/kb/start/get-started#what-does-the-getting-started-chat-cost) until that chat has used 2 US
dollars of AI (40 credits). What those turns start, such as an AI search, Find people or a paid
column, is charged as usual. A read answered from saved data is free at both Enrich levels. Some labels in the app
still print the figures from before 26 November 2026 (an Enrich level at 1 or 3 a row, "1 credit per company
searched"): the wallet takes the prices above, and a cell's **Log** and **Settings**, **Usage** show what was charged.
The details of each price are in
[Add people and companies](/kb/tables/add-people-and-companies#which-links-does-an-ai-search-take-and-what-does-it-cost)
(AI search and [post engagement](/kb/tables/add-people-and-companies#what-does-a-post-engagement-bring-and-what-does-it-cost)),
[Find people at companies](/kb/tables/find-people-at-companies#what-does-find-people-cost),
[Find emails and enrich](/kb/tables/find-emails-and-enrich) and [AI columns](/kb/tables/ai-columns).

## What caps credit spend before the wallet is empty?

Four brakes cap credit spend, each with its own number:

- A run started from a table stops at its cap, the estimate's maximum: "Run stopped at its budget".
- A column's daily credit limit stops it for the day (**Daily credit limit** in the drawer of a Find people, Latest
  posts or API tool call column, `config.daily_credit_limit` over the API for the others). A column that runs on new
  rows waits and goes on after 00:00 UTC.
- The AI SDR spends at most 40 credits on a thread by default, then hands the thread to you.
- A play's **Daily ceiling** (25 to 2,500 credits, or **No ceiling**) caps what it spends in a day
  ([Signals](/kb/signals/signals)).

## What happens when credits run out?

When the wallet is empty, paid work stops and nothing is charged for what was refused:

- A column's cell reads **Out of credits** and the run stops.
- A data source pauses: "The workspace ran out of credits, so the source paused. Top up, then resume."
- Creator answers "The workspace is out of credits": "Nothing was changed. Top up the credits, then send the message
  again."
- The AI SDR stops writing: "The workspace is out of credits. Top up to let the agent go on, or do this step yourself."

Sending does not use credits, so workflow steps that only send keep going. **Top up**, and resume.

## Where do I see what the credits went on?

**Settings**, then **Usage** (https://app.getsales.io/settings/usage), open to every member, shows what spent the
workspace's credits: by product (Playtables, AI SDR, Creator, Other), by table, by sender profile and by person,
for **This period**, **Last 30 days** or **Last 90 days**, with a chart by day and **Export** as CSV. Each feature
has its own line: "Work emails found", "Work emails checked", "Use AI columns", "AI search", "Find people",
"Enrichment", "Replies written", "Chats" and the rest.

Over the API or MCP: [`get_credit_transactions_metrics`](/api-reference/id/credit_transactions/credit-spend-by-day).

## How do I buy or change a plan?

Plans are bought and changed in **Settings**, then **Subscription** (https://app.getsales.io/settings/subscription).
Seeing it needs the Billing View permission, and buying or changing needs Billing Manage.

**To buy**, click **Buy resources** (on the Free plan or with no plan) or **Buy now** (on the trial):

1. Pick the **Currency** and the **Period**.
2. Pick the **Data subscription** (a new order opens on **Startup**), set the **LinkedIn sender profiles** and the
   **Email inbox capacity**, switch on **The AI SDR for this workspace** if you want it, and add **Vendor credits**
   if you want them. Below 188 LinkedIn accounts, the window names the next bracket (at 3 accounts: "Add 2 and every
   account costs $39: $195 a month in all.", with **Take 5**) and, when the bigger count costs less, what it saves
   (at 4 accounts: "4 at $236 against 5 at $195: $41 a month saved."). The totals show **Monthly**, **One time**,
   **Due now** and the **Next payment**, and the service the plan includes or how much more a month reaches it (with
   Startup, 3 accounts and 100 inboxes, $265 a month: "$235 a month more reaches Business service, white label
   included.").
3. A promo code goes under **Have a code?**: it takes its discount off the lines it covers, never off vendor
   credits ("This code does not take money off vendor credits. Take them out of the order to use it."), and a
   workspace uses a code once.
4. Click **Continue to payment** and pay in the payment provider's window. "Payment received. The plan activates in a
   moment."

A workspace already on a paid plan cannot buy a second one: "This workspace already runs on a paid subscription.
Change that plan instead of buying a second one." Vendor credits are added to a paid plan with **Top up**.

**To change**, click **Change plan** and move the data subscription, the LinkedIn sender profiles, the inbox package
or the AI SDR, then **Confirm change**. The window previews every step before anything is billed:

- A bigger data subscription is charged now, prorated. A smaller one applies from the next period.
- An added AI SDR license is charged now, prorated. A removed one stays until the end of the period.
- More LinkedIn sender profiles are charged now, prorated. Fewer are prorated too, and what you are owed becomes a
  credit on later invoices, never a refund to the card.
- An added inbox package is charged now, prorated, and brings its email server, usually ready within minutes.
  A removed one stays until the end of the period, with its server and the mailboxes on it. Then the server is
  paused, and nothing on it is deleted without a mail from GetSales first.
- A workspace holds one inbox package of one size. **To change its size, take another size in its place** in
  **Change plan**: the package changes size and the email server changes with every mailbox on it, never a second
  package. Change plan offers the sizes your package can change to; for a size it does not offer, write to support.
  A larger size inside 100, 500 and 2,000 grows the same server, with a few minutes of pause; a change to or from
  10,000, and any smaller size, moves the mailboxes to a new server of the size with everything in them, paused while
  their data is copied, from a few minutes to a few hours. The window's summary says what the change costs and when
  it takes effect. A smaller size the connected mailboxes do not fit is refused before anything is billed ("112
  mailboxes are connected, and a package of 100 inboxes holds 100. Detach 12 first, then change the size.").
- A plan bought before 26 November 2026 has no data subscription and runs on the Startup limits free. Its first
  change asks for one: "Your plan has the Startup limits free. A change of plan asks for a data subscription, and
  the free limits end with it." Monthly credits on such a plan can only be lowered here, from the next period: more
  credits come with **Top up**.
- The currency, the period and the renewal date do not change. Removing every line is refused: cancel instead.

<Note>
A subscription is paid by the person who bought it: their card is charged, and the receipts go to them ("Receipts and
payment notices go to the person who pays for this plan"). The buyer, and every member holding Billing Manage in the
workspace the plan feeds, can change, pause or cancel it. A member with Billing View alone sees the plan and "Only a
member who manages billing in this workspace can change this plan." Each workspace has a plan of its own: splitting
one subscription across workspaces is open to partner accounts only, and such a subscription is changed by its buyer
alone ("This workspace holds a share of a subscription its buyer manages. Ask them to change the plan.").
</Note>

Over the API or MCP: [`change_billing_subscription`](/api-reference/id/billing_subscriptions/change-the-plan:-data-subscription-linkedin-inboxes-ai-sdr-credits),
after a priced preview, for the buyer or anyone holding Billing Manage in the workspace the plan feeds. Without
Billing Manage the answer is `403` `scope_missing`, and on a subscription a partner splits across workspaces anyone
but its buyer gets `403` `not_subscription_owner`. It takes `data_subscription` and `ai_sdr` beside the LinkedIn and
inbox lines, and never buys vendor credits (`422` `vendor_credits_by_checkout`: a checkout in the app buys them).

## What happens to an email server nobody pays for?

An email server whose package is no longer paid for (the payment failed past its 3 days of grace, the plan is
paused, or the package left the plan) is paused: its mailboxes do not sync and nothing is sent from them. A mail
then names the moment it is deleted with every mailbox on it, seven days ahead; a deleted server cannot be
restored. Two more mails follow, three days before that moment and the day before, and one more once the server is
gone. They go to the workspace's owner, and to whoever pays for its plan when they are a member of the workspace,
and each names the moment in UTC. Paid for again before it, the server comes back as it was, with everything on it.
**Add mailbox** shows the day on a paused server.

## Where are my invoices and receipts?

**Settings**, then **Invoices** (https://app.getsales.io/settings/invoices), open with the Billing View permission,
lists the charges you paid, across all your workspaces, and the charges made for this workspace: **Charge**
(Subscription, Plan change or One-time charge, with the invoice number), **Date**, **Amount** and **Status** (Paid,
Completed, Billed, Past due, Canceled). **Open invoice** opens the invoice PDF in a new tab. A charge that was never
billed, such as the $0 charge of a card change, has no invoice.

Every completed charge also sends "Your payment receipt" to the billing email, the paying account's own address:
"Receipts and payment notices go here, not to the workspace."

The app holds no company name, VAT ID or postal address for an invoice: they are entered in the payment provider's
checkout.

Over the API or MCP: [`search_billing_transactions`](/api-reference/id/billing_transactions/search-billing-transactions)
and [`get_billing_transaction_invoice`](/api-reference/id/billing_transactions/get-transaction-invoice).

## How do I update the card?

In **Settings**, **Subscription**, the **Billing information** card has **Update card**. It opens the payment
provider's page in a new tab, where you change the card the plan bills. The link works for about an hour. Back in
GetSales, the panel reads the cards again. The card that bills a live plan cannot be removed: add another one first.
A trial has a card only when one was added to it, and a plan billed by invoice has no card to update.

Over the API or MCP: [`get_billing_payment_method_add_link`](/api-reference/id/billing_payment_methods/get-add-card-link)
returns the same link.

## What happens when a payment fails?

A failed renewal turns the subscription **Past due**, and every workspace on it keeps working for 3 days from the
failure. The payment provider retries the payment by itself.

1. **At the failure**: everything keeps running. The person who pays gets "Payment failed: update your card by" the
   date. Every page shows a banner, "The last payment failed.", to members with the Billing View permission (the
   workspace's owner and Superadmin hold it, the Operator and View only presets do not). Members with Billing Manage
   also get **Retry payment**, which opens the payment page for the buyer and for anyone holding Billing Manage in the
   workspace the plan feeds.
2. **24 hours before the grace ends**: one reminder, "One day left".
3. **After 3 days**: every workspace on the subscription is suspended until the payment lands. Its tables,
   workflows, Messages and Signals, and the API, answer `402` `subscription_required`, while Settings stays open
   except its **Stoplist**, **Pipeline stages** and **Website** panels, which answer `402` too. Sends and workflows
   wait, and the LinkedIn accounts and mailboxes are parked. The data and the credits are kept, but no monthly
   credits are granted for an unpaid period. Each workspace's owner gets "(the workspace's name) is suspended: the
   payment is still due".

**Retry payment** and **Update card** open the payment provider's page to fix the card, and do not charge by
themselves. Once the payment lands, the workspaces come back by themselves, with the period's credits. If the
payment provider gives up and ends the subscription, the workspaces move to the Free plan, and the plan's credits are
lost.

## How do I pause or cancel a plan?

Both are on the subscription card in **Settings**, **Subscription**, open to the buyer and to Billing Manage in the
workspace the plan feeds, and both take effect at the end of the current period: nothing changes until then.

- **Cancel plan**: "Every workspace on this subscription moves to the Free plan then: Playtables and workflows keep
  working, LinkedIn and email stop, and the plan's credits expire." **Undo cancel** takes it back before the date.
  A plan with an inbox package says what its email server does at that date, and the mail that the plan ended says it
  again.
  The plan cannot be changed while a cancel is scheduled. GetSales gives no refunds.
- **Pause**: "Every workspace on this subscription is suspended until you resume; sender profiles and data are
  held." The credits wait too. A pause has no time limit. **Resume** starts the plan again, and **Don't pause**
  takes back a pause that has not started.

<Warning>
Deleting a workspace does not cancel its plan, and a standard account then has no screen left to cancel it from.
Cancel the plan first, then delete the workspace. Deleting your own account is refused while a paid subscription is
live: "Cancel it under Billing > Subscription, then try again."
</Warning>

## What does GetSales billing not do?

- It does not charge per team member, per message or per connection request.
- It does not refund a payment or the rest of a period.
- It does not top up credits by itself: credits are added with **Top up**.
- It does not keep a workflow's LinkedIn and email steps waiting on the Free plan: they fail as they come due, and go
  out only when you run them again once a plan covers the accounts.
- It does not let a standard account split one plan across several workspaces.
- It does not turn credits back into money.
- It does not buy, pause, resume or cancel a plan over the API or MCP: those are buttons in **Settings**,
  **Subscription**, open to Billing Manage. The API reads and quotes a plan, and changes it for the buyer or for
  anyone holding Billing Manage in the workspace the plan feeds. It buys no vendor credits either: a checkout in the
  app does.
