A data source (`data_source`) brings people or companies into a table in bulk. It sits on the table's
**Data sources** tab and gives every record it brings a status: imported, or the reason it was not. A
row can also come in without a data source, and then has no such status: added by hand (**Add** N **more
rows at the bottom** under the grid's last row, or **Add people**, **Add a person** on a company's card), moved from
another table, or created with [`create_person`](/api-reference/playtables/people/create-person) or
[`create_company`](/api-reference/playtables/companies/create-company).

<Tip>
**Give this to your AI assistant** (with the [GetSales connector](/kb/ai/connect-your-ai-assistant)):

"Using the GetSales connector, fill my table TABLE NAME with WHO YOU SELL TO. Plan the AI search filter
first and show it to me with what each page of results costs, and wait for my yes before you create the
source. If I give you a LinkedIn search link instead, create a sender search on my sender profile
SENDER NAME. Once it runs, tell me its status, the page it has reached, and how many records were
imported, duplicate or stoplisted."
</Tip>

## Which data source should I use?

A table is filled in bulk by data sources, and the kind of source follows from what you start with:
words, a search link, posts, a file, another tool or a list of your CRM. Open the table, click **Data
sources**, then one of **AI search**, **Sender search**, **Post engagement**, **CSV**, **Webhook** or
**CRM segment**. The data source `type` decides who runs it and what it costs.

| What you have | Source (`type`) | Credits |
|---|---|---|
| Words for who you sell to, or a Sales Navigator search link | **AI search** (`ai_search`) | 0.5 a page that returns somebody, plus building the filter from words |
| A LinkedIn or Sales Navigator search, a saved search, a list, a Recruiter search | **Sender search** (`sender_search`) on your sender profile | Free |
| A sender profile's connections, conversations or incoming invitations | **Sender search**: **My network**, **My conversations**, **My invitations** | Free |
| A post, a person's posts, a company page's posts, or a keyword | **Post engagement** (`post_engagement`) | Free when one of your sender profiles reads it, credits otherwise. **Only relevant posts** costs credits |
| A spreadsheet of people or companies | **CSV** (`csv`) | A fraction of a credit a file to match its columns, and the rows are free |
| A tool that can send a webhook | **Webhook** (`webhook`) | Free |
| A HubSpot segment, a Pipedrive saved filter, a Salesforce campaign or list view, or a folk group | **CRM segment** (`crm_list`) | Free |

[Check on save](#what-does-check-on-save-do), when you switch it on, adds one AI call per 20 records to
any of them but a CRM segment. The seventh kind, `find_people`, finds people at companies: see
[Find people at companies](/kb/tables/find-people-at-companies).

The tab lists every source with its **Status**, what it **Imported** (the people or companies it added,
which open the table's grid, then its errors, duplicates, stoplisted and disqualified records, each count
opening them in **Import results**), the
**Sender profile** that runs it (**Credits** for an AI search and Find people, and for a post
engagement once our managed infrastructure has read posts for it) and the day it was **Created**.

## How do I find people or companies with an AI search?

An AI search turns your words into one search filter and reads its results page by page into the
table, with no sender profile and no account of yours to connect.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe who to find">
    Click **AI search**. In **Describe who to find, or paste a link**, write who you want in your own
    words, up to 2,000 characters, for example "Finance leaders at EU and US SaaS companies, 50 to
    500 people". Or paste a Sales Navigator people or company search link: a link runs as it is.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say what to find">
    Under **What to find**, pick **People** or **Companies**. A pasted link sets it for you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the source">
    Add a name and tags if you like, switch on [Check on save](#what-does-check-on-save-do) if only
    some records should get in, then click **Create source**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="The AI search dialog with a description of finance leaders at EU and US SaaS companies, What to find on People, and Check on save switched on, its cost shown before Create source: About 0.24 credits per 20 records.">
  <img src="/images/kb/tables/add-ai-search-dialog.png" alt="AI search dialog. What to find is set to People, not Companies. Data source name and Tags (optional) are empty. The source reads **Building the search filter** while AI turns the words into a filter, then
**Searching: page 3** as results land a page at a time (**Searching: page 3, retrying** while a page is
tried again): 25 people or companies a page, up to 100 pages, 2,500 at most. It keeps going with the app
closed and ends **Completed**. An AI search runs once: for new results later, create a new one. The
words cannot change once the filter is built, since a different description is a new source.

## Which links does an AI search take, and what does it cost?

An AI search takes your words, or a Sales Navigator people or company search link, and costs 0.5 credit
for each page of results that returns somebody.

A LinkedIn search link, a saved search, a list, a Recruiter search and the network and inbox pages run
as a sender search on your sender profile instead, and the dialog says so: **Save as sender search**
opens a sender search with the link filled in. A post link gets **Open in Post engagement**.

<Note>
A search link can be up to 15,000 characters, in an AI search and in a sender search, and it is never
cut. A longer one is refused with **This link is too long to save. Save the search in Sales Navigator and
paste the saved search's link.** (`link_too_long`): save the search in Sales Navigator on the LinkedIn
account of the sender profile that will run it and paste the saved search's link into a sender search.
Or remove some filters, or describe the people in your words instead.
</Note>

An AI search costs 0.5 credit for each page that returns somebody and nothing for a page that returns
nobody. Building the filter is one AI call, plus 0.1 credit for each term it has to look up, such as a
place or an industry (a term already looked up in the past week, or one that matches nothing, costs
nothing).

API and MCP: [`plan_data_source_filters`](/api-reference/playtables/data_sources/plan-data-source-filters)
builds and shows the filter before anything is created, and is paid like a source's own filter (the AI
call and the term lookups).
[`preview_company_search`](/api-reference/playtables/data_sources/preview-company-search) plans a description
of companies and returns up to 25 of the first page with the filters to create the source with: nothing is
created, and it is paid like the plan and one page. `create_data_source` with `type: ai_search` and `payload.prompt` starts the
search.

## How do I collect a LinkedIn, Sales Navigator or Recruiter search on my sender profile?

A sender search reads a link or a list through one of your sender profiles, on your own LinkedIn
account, and costs no credits.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the sender profile">
    Click **Sender search** and choose a **Sender profile**. Only sender profiles with a LinkedIn
    account are listed, and **SN** and **Recruiter** mark the licences they hold.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say what to collect">
    Under **What to collect**, pick **Paste a link** and paste a LinkedIn search, a Sales Navigator
    search, a saved search, a list or a Recruiter search: what it is gets read off the link. Or pick
    **My network** (the sender's 1st-degree connections), **My conversations** or **My invitations**,
    with no link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the interval">
    **Interval** is **Once** by default. **Every hour**, **Every day**, **Every week** or **Every
    month** runs it again: see [how a source runs on a schedule](#how-does-a-data-source-run-again-on-a-schedule).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How long does a sender search take, and what does it need?

A sender search reads its pages within a daily search allowance of the sender profile's LinkedIn account,
which is not one of the rows under **Auto tasks and daily limits**, so a long search takes days: when the
day's allowance is spent it waits and goes on from the page it stopped at. Each run reads up to 100 pages
of a search, and a Recruiter search up to 40 pages of 25 people: split a bigger search into narrower
ones.

A Sales Navigator link needs a sender profile with Sales Navigator (`sales_nav_required` otherwise),
and a Recruiter link one with a Recruiter licence (`recruiter_required`) and a live Recruiter session
(`recruiter_signed_out`: sign in to Recruiter again in the sender's browser). A Recruiter link must
carry its search ID: run the search in Recruiter, then copy the address again, with `searchHistoryId`
in it. A Recruiter search that loses the licence or the session while it runs pauses with
`recruiter_required` or `recruiter_reauth_required`: fix it, then click **Resume import**.

API and MCP: `create_data_source` with `type: sender_search`, `sender_profile_sid` and `url`, or
`payload.surface` set to `my_network`, `messenger` or `my_invitations`, and `payload.repeat` sets the
interval.

## How do I collect the people who engaged with posts?

A post engagement source collects the people who reacted to, commented on or reposted posts, each
with what they did.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Say what to watch">
    Click **Post engagement**. Under **What to watch**, pick **A post**, **A person** or **A company**
    and paste the link of the post, the profile or the company page. Or pick **A keyword** and type 2
    to 200 characters: quotes keep a phrase together, and AND, OR and NOT combine words.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the window and what to collect">
    **Posts from** takes 7, 30 or 90 days or **Since a date** for a person or a company, and 24 hours,
    7 or 30 days for a keyword, and a single post has none. **Collect** has **Reactions** and
    **Comments** on and **Reposts** off. **Only relevant posts** has AI read each post first and
    skip the ones your words rule out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the interval">
    A post runs **Once** by default. A person, a company or a keyword runs **Every day**, reading the
    new posts of the window and the new people on posts already read.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What does a post engagement bring, and what does it cost?

A post engagement brings its people with the columns **Engagement**, **Comment**, **Post**, **Post
author** and **Post text**. A run reads up to 100 posts and everyone who engaged with each. The read is
free when one of your sender profiles can read posts. When none can, our managed infrastructure reads
them for credits: 0.5 for each page of 50 people, and 0.1 to 0.5 for each read that finds the posts, and the
dialog shows that cost before you create the source. **Only relevant posts** costs credits either way:
one AI call for each 20 posts it judges.

API and MCP: [`preview_post_engagement`](/api-reference/playtables/data_sources/preview-post-engagement)
reads a link or a keyword back for free over the API and in Creator. An assistant at the public MCP
address lacks it, because the tool reads LinkedIn itself. `create_data_source` with
`type: post_engagement` creates the source at every address, the public one included.

## How do I import a CSV file?

A CSV import adds or updates people or companies from a `.csv` file of up to 50 MB.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Drop the file">
    Click **CSV** and drop the file. A file over 50 MB is refused: split it and import the parts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say what it is and what to do">
    Under **What to import**, pick people or companies. Under **Action**, **Create new** makes every
    row a new record, and rows that already exist follow the duplicate setting. **Update existing**
    updates the record each row's match key finds: pick **Match by** (email or LinkedIn nickname for people, and domain, LinkedIn nickname or name for companies) and the **Match column**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the identifiers and the mapping">
    **Found identifiers** lists the columns that say who each row is: a LinkedIn profile, an email, a
    domain, a LinkedIn page. Without a LinkedIn identifier, LinkedIn steps cannot reach the file's
    people. **Map columns** matches each column for you: check it before you import. An unmapped
    column is still imported and can become a column later from a row's **Data source** cell.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import">
    Click **Import**. Rows land in batches while you keep working.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What are the limits of a CSV import?

A CSV import takes a `.csv` file of up to 50 MB (a bigger file is refused: split it and import the
parts) and has no row limit, but a single row larger than 1 MB fails. With **Update existing**, a row
whose key matches nothing fails and never creates a record. Matching the columns costs a fraction of a
credit for the whole file, and the rows are free.

API and MCP: [`create_data_source_upload_url`](/api-reference/playtables/data_sources/create-data-source-upload-url),
then [`import_data_source`](/api-reference/playtables/data_sources/import-data-source) with the
mapping and `mode`.

## How do I send rows from another tool by webhook?

A webhook source turns every JSON object posted to its own address into a person in the table.

Click **Webhook**, and when the records sit inside the body, open **Show advanced settings** and fill
**Records path** (for example `data.contacts`). Left empty, a body that is one JSON object is one record
and a body that is a JSON array is one record per object. Then click **Create source**. Copy the
**Endpoint URL** and send a `POST` with JSON to it from any tool. Name and email are detected, and the
whole payload stays on the row. Copy the address again any time with **Copy webhook URL** in the
source's menu.

<Warning>
One call takes up to 100 records, each up to 1 MB. A call over either limit is refused whole and
nothing from it is saved. A paused source refuses calls too. **Rotate webhook URL** retires the old
address at once, so update every tool that posts to it.
</Warning>

API and MCP: `create_data_source` with `type: webhook` answers the `ingest_url`, and [`rotate_data_source_ingest_key`](/api-reference/playtables/data_sources/rotate-data-source-ingest-key)
mints a new one.

## How do I sync a CRM list into a table?

A CRM segment source keeps a table's people in step with one list of your CRM: a HubSpot segment, a
Pipedrive saved filter of persons, a Salesforce campaign or a lead or contact list view, or a folk group.

Click **CRM segment**, pick the **CRM account** and the list (**Segment**, **Saved filter**, **Campaign or
list view** or **Group**), and optionally up to 25 of its fields as columns. On Pipedrive and Salesforce,
**LinkedIn field** names the field that holds a person's LinkedIn link. **Sync** reads the list every hour
by default, or every day or week, and **Sync now** in the source's menu reads it at once. A person added to
the list is added to the table, and one who leaves it leaves the table's workflows and stays in the table.
Connect the CRM first: [Connect your CRM](/kb/integrations/connect-your-crm).

API and MCP: [`create_data_source`](/api-reference/playtables/data_sources/create-data-source) with
`type: crm_list` and `payload.connection_sid`, `list_id` and `list_name`, and
[`sync_data_source`](/api-reference/playtables/data_sources/sync-data-source-now) reads the list at once,
as **Sync now** does.

## How does a data source run again on a schedule?

A sender search, a post engagement and a CRM segment run again on their interval. An AI search and
a CSV run once, and a webhook listens all the time.

**Interval**, on a sender search and a post engagement, offers **Once**, **Every hour**, **Every day**,
**Every week** and **Every month** (`repeat`: `hourly`, `daily`, `weekly`, `monthly`, and none for
**Once**), and **View details** changes it later. A CRM segment's **Sync** offers **Every hour** (the
default), **Every day** and **Every week**. A repeating source's row shows its interval between two runs,
and hovering it shows **Next run on** with the date (**View details** shows **Next run**). A repeating
LinkedIn or Sales Navigator search, saved search or list keeps its own schedule: its row shows neither,
and **View details** shows its **Interval** with no **Next run**.

A run never adds the same person twice: a repeating source stages only what its earlier runs did not
bring (a Recruiter search reads everyone again), and every record goes through the workspace's duplicate
check, so a person already in the workspace ends `duplicate` and only new people become rows.

## What does Check on save do?

Check on save has AI judge every new record against your own words before it is saved, so only the
records that pass become rows. Switch it on in the source's dialog and say who passes, for example "a
decision maker at a marketing agency, not a freelancer".

- The check sees only what the source brings, such as the name, headline, company and location. A
  record it cannot judge is saved.
- A record that fails ends `disqualified` (**Disqualified**) with the AI's reason, and this table
  refuses the same identity again without another check. People already in the workspace are not
  checked.
- The cost shows under the switch before you save: one AI call per 20 records, **About N credits per
  N records**.
- Check on save is off on a new source and can be changed later in the source's **View details**.
  AI search, sender search, post engagement, CSV and webhook sources have it, and CRM segments do not.

API and MCP: the `qualify` field of `create_data_source`, `{on, criterion}`.

## What happens to each record a data source brings?

Every record a data source brings ends with a status, listed in **Import results**: click a count on the
source's row, such as **2 duplicates** or **1 error**, then **All** for every record. **View imported** in
the source's menu opens the table's **People** or **Companies** tab instead.

| Status | Item `status` | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| **Imported** | `promoted` | A new row of this table |
| **Duplicate** | `duplicate` | Already in a table of this workspace, which **Import results** names. It stays there |
| **Moved here** | `duplicate`, with `duplicate_action: moved` | Was in another table and moved here, by **Move here if already saved** or later by **Move here** or **Move all N into this table** |
| **Stoplisted** | `stoplisted` | Matches your stoplist: the row is created, marked, and never contacted |
| **Disqualified** | `disqualified` | Failed Check on save |
| **Failed** | `failed` | Could not be imported, and the result says why |
| **Pending** | `pending` | Waiting its turn: the source's row counts it as **Indexing** N |

**Move here if already saved**, under **Show advanced settings**, is off on a new source
(`on_duplicate: skip`, and `move` when it is on). A person moved here leaves the other table: their
values in its columns are cleared and their unfinished journeys in its workflows end, while their
pipeline stage, owner and history come with them. To move duplicates later, click the source's
duplicates count to open **Import results**: a duplicate left where it is has **Move here** on its row,
and a banner above the list counts the people already in other tables, by table, with **Move all N into
this table**. Companies never move: an existing company joins this table as well. A person's company
joins this table's **Companies** tab. The source's tags go on every new row, and **Add tags to
duplicates** puts them on records that already existed too.

API and MCP: [`search_data_source_items`](/api-reference/playtables/data_source_items/search-data-source-items),
[`get_data_sources_metrics`](/api-reference/playtables/data_sources/data-sources-metrics), and

## Why did a data source stop, and how do I resume it?

A data source shows **Draft**, **Running**, **Listening** (a webhook waiting for calls), **Paused**,
**Completed** or **Failed** (`status`: `draft`, `active`, `paused`, `completed`, `failed`). A paused or
failed source says why in **View details**, under **Why it stopped**, in a sentence, and the API's
`status_reason` holds the reason's token:

| Reason | What happened | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| `allowance_exhausted` | The workspace ran out of credits | Top up, then **Resume import**: the same page runs again |
| `collect_interrupted` | The search kept getting interrupted, for hours | **Resume import** goes on from where it left off |
| `collect_failed` | This filter could not be run | Resume once, or create a new source with a different description |
| `filter_unresolved` | Some words matched no search filter, so the search did not start | Resume to search without them, or use other words in a new source |
| `plan_failed` | The words could not be turned into a filter | Edit the description and resume |
| `sales_nav_required` | An AI search paused: the search is not available right now | Resume later |
| `executor_account_missing` | The sender profile has no LinkedIn account connected | Connect one, then resume |
| `recruiter_required`, `recruiter_reauth_required` | A Recruiter search paused: the sender profile's account has no Recruiter licence, or its Recruiter session ended. **View details** shows the token itself | Get the licence, or sign in to Recruiter again in the sender's browser, then resume |
| A sentence about the sender's LinkedIn account | The account was not available, could not start or run the search, or the search failed several times in a row | Check the sender profile ([When a sender profile needs attention](/kb/sender-profiles/troubleshooting)), then **Resume import** |
| `post_unavailable` | The post could not be read: deleted or private | Check the link, or create a source with another post |
| `target_not_found` | LinkedIn has no page at that profile or company page address any more | Create a source with another link |
| `file_unreadable`, `parse_failed` | The uploaded file could not be read, or stopped loading part way through | Upload the file again |
| `crm_list_scope_missing` | The CRM account cannot read this list yet (a HubSpot portal connected before segments could be read) | Connect it again in **Settings**, **Integrations**, then resume |
| `crm_list_unreadable` | The CRM refused the list: it was deleted or is not a list of contacts | Pick it again in a new source |
| `playtable_archived` | The table was archived | Resume once the table is back in use |

Nothing resumes by itself after a top-up: press **Resume import** in the source's menu. The one
exception is a Find people search, which starts again as soon as its Find people column (once there are
credits) or a find people play hands it a new company. The source's menu also has **View details**,
**View imported** (the table's **People** or **Companies** tab), **Pause import**, **Sync now** for
a CRM segment, **Copy webhook URL** and **Rotate webhook URL** for a webhook, and **Delete source**. Deleting
a source keeps the people and companies it imported.

API and MCP: [`update_data_source`](/api-reference/playtables/data_sources/update-data-source) with
`status: active` or `paused`, and [`delete_data_source`](/api-reference/playtables/data_sources/delete-data-source).

## Why are rows not coming into my table?

Rows usually stay out because the data source paused or failed, because the records came in with
another status, or because a sender search is waiting on its sender profile. Check these in order:

1. **The source is paused or failed.** Open **View details** and read **Why it stopped**: the common reasons and their fixes are in [Why did a data source stop](#why-did-a-data-source-stop-and-how-do-i-resume-it).
2. **The records came in with another status.** Click the source's duplicates, stoplisted or
   disqualified count: **Import results** shows the duplicates, which stay in their own table, and the
   stoplisted and disqualified records with what matched.
3. **A sender search is waiting.** It reads within the daily search allowance of its sender profile's
   LinkedIn account, and waits while that account is not available. **My network**,
   **My conversations** and **My invitations** also wait, still **Running**, until the account's first
   sync of that list has finished. See [When a sender profile needs attention](/kb/sender-profiles/troubleshooting).
4. **The link belongs to another kind.** A LinkedIn search, a saved search, a list and a Recruiter
   search run as a sender search, and a post runs as post engagement.
5. **The table is archived.** Its sources pause until it is back in use.

API and MCP: [`search_data_source_items`](/api-reference/playtables/data_source_items/search-data-source-items)
with a `status` filter lists a source's records of one status.

## What does adding people not do?

A data source does not do these things:

- Adding people to a table does not by itself put them into a workflow: a workflow's sources do. A
  running workflow of this table with a **From a view** or **By filter** source and **Auto-add** on
  checks every 10 minutes and takes in the new people who match, and they start its steps. See
  [Build an outreach workflow](/kb/workflows/build-a-workflow#how-do-auto-add-and-a-workflow-source%E2%80%99s-limits-work).
- An AI search does not run again by itself, and a source paused for credits does not resume by itself,
  except a Find people search, which starts again when its Find people column (once there are credits)
  or a find people play hands it a new company.
- A CSV in **Update existing** never creates a record, and a webhook source adds people only.
- Check on save never judges people who are already in the workspace.
- No data source puts a person in two tables of the workspace.
